# Prediction Markets and Fed Policy Forecasting

## Research Question

How accurately do prediction markets forecast Federal Reserve monetary policy decisions?

## Objective

This project evaluates the forecasting accuracy of prediction markets for Federal Reserve policy decisions and compares their performance with traditional financial market indicators.

## Data

The analysis will use:

* Prediction market data
* Federal Reserve policy decisions
* Federal Funds Rate data from FRED
* Federal Funds Futures data

## Methodology

The project will use:

* Forecast error analysis
* Mean Absolute Error (MAE)
* Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE)
* OLS regression
* Event study analysis

## Research Focus

The analysis focuses on whether prediction market prices contain useful information about future monetary policy decisions and how their forecasting performance compares with traditional market based measures.
