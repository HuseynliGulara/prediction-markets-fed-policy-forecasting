# Fed Language and Financial Markets

## Research Question

Does the tone of FOMC statements predict movements in Treasury yields and market volatility?

## Objective

This project examines whether the language used in Federal Open Market Committee statements contains information about subsequent movements in financial markets.

## Data

The analysis will combine:

* FOMC statements
* 2 Year Treasury Yield
* 10 Year Treasury Yield
* VIX
* Federal Funds Rate

## Methodology

* Natural Language Processing
* Sentiment and tone measures
* Event study analysis
* OLS regression
* Out of sample analysis

## Research Focus

The project examines whether changes in the language of FOMC statements are associated with subsequent movements in Treasury yields and market volatility.
